MCF JARVIS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

MCF JARVIS is a personal tool used only by the owner of the "Mancos con Fe" channel to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account.

What data it uses

  • From TikTok, only the owner's own account: basic profile information (display name and avatar) and the posting settings TikTok returns (for example the allowed privacy options and the maximum video length). This is used only to show the posting screen before each post.
  • Access tokens for the owner's account. They are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.
  • It does not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not send the data to third parties.

Where data is kept

  • Everything runs on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on external servers controlled by the tool.

Deleting data

  • The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions). The local tokens are then deleted.

Contact

  • Through the TikTok account @mancosconfe.
