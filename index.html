MCF JARVIS

MCF JARVIS is the publishing assistant of the Mancos con Fe gaming channel (@mancosconfe). It helps the channel owner prepare, review and schedule short Counter-Strike 2 gameplay clips and send them to TikTok.

How MCF JARVIS works with TikTok

  1. Login Kit - the channel owner connects the channel's TikTok account to MCF JARVIS (scope user.info.basic).
  2. Content Posting API - a finished clip is uploaded to TikTok as a draft, or posted after the owner reviews it and confirms the title, privacy setting and options on the posting screen (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  3. Nothing is published without an explicit confirmation from the channel owner.

MCF JARVIS is operated only by the owner of the channel. It is not offered to other users.

Channel: Mancos con Fe (@mancosconfe).
